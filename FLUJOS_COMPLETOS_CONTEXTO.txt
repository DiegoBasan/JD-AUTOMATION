SECTION 1: CRITICAL MATERIALS & PROCESSES
================================================================================

DELLO (Adhesive) - CRITICAL BOTTLENECK
- Supplied in tube, stored in refrigerator
- Requires 1.5 hours ambient preparation before use
- Duration per use: Variable (typically 8 pieces in CPK, then variable)
- Consumption rate: Depends on dispensing parameters and environmental conditions
- If spilled or lost: Ambient preparation timer resets (1.5 hrs)
- Critical safety: Compressed air must be shut off and opresor secured during DELLO tube changes
- Dispensing equipment: Kasalis (sensor assembly) and ASELEC (housing assembly)

SENSOR ASSEMBLY OVEN CYCLE
- Temperature and duration: Per specification (varies by product line)
- Purpose: Full curing of DELLO adhesive on sensor/lens adapter assembly
- Timing: Occurs after Kasalis BUILD phase, before RETEST validation
- Status: Passive (no operator intervention required)

HOUSING ASSEMBLY OVEN CYCLE
- Separate oven from sensor assembly cycle
- Temperature and duration: Per specification (varies by product line)
- Purpose: Full curing of DELLO adhesive in housing assembly
- Timing: Occurs after ASELEC processing, before Final Assy
- Status: Passive (no operator intervention required)

LENS ADAPTER PLASMA TREATMENT PROCESS
- Occurs pre-assembly (before sensor attachment)
- Step 1: Ultrasonic cleaning of lens adapter
- Step 2: Plasma treatment of lens adapter top surface
- Purpose: Surface preparation to enable DELLO adhesion
- Critical: Plasma creates micro-surface texture that allows DELLO molecular bonding
- Output: Lens adapter ready for sensor attachment and subsequent Kasalis processing

KASALIS EQUIPMENT - CORE OPTICAL ASSEMBLY
- Two machines: Kasalis Scorpion (Scorpion line only), Kasalis ELK (ELK + Moose contingency)
- Function - BUILD mode:
  * Dispenses DELLO on lens adapter
  * Performs optical alignment via SFR search (pick-and-place of lens)
  * Applies UV cure (2-5 min)
  * Output: Sensor assembly ready for oven curing
- Function - RETEST mode:
  * Validates SFR after oven curing (post-ambient cure)
  * Confirms DELLO did not shift during oven process
  * Pass/Fail outcomes documented for quality tracking

SFR (SPATIAL FREQUENCY RESPONSE) - OPTICAL QUALITY METRIC
- Measurement: Optical resolution and focus quality of lens-sensor assembly
- Target: Variable by lens model (76° vs 95° vs 131° vs 133.4° have different targets)
- Validation points: Kasalis BUILD phase and Kasalis RETEST phase
- Failure code 161: Does not reach SFR target (causes: dirty targets, dirty lens, communication issues)
- Failure code 155: SFR drops post-oven (suggests gripper movement or DELLO shift)
- Failure code 108: Assembly contaminated pre-cure

ASELEC EQUIPMENT - AUTOMATED HOUSING PROCESSING
- Dispenses specialized DELLO in housing
- Performs plasma treatment of housing surface
- Performs pick-and-place of glass window into housing
- Output: Housing assembly ready for oven curing
- Critical: All housing-related DELLO and glass operations consolidated in single machine

SECTION 7: FAILURE MODES & RECOVERY
================================================================================

CODE 161: DOES NOT REACH SFR TARGET
- Kasalis BUILD: Optical alignment cannot find acceptable focus position
- Kasalis RETEST: Post-cure SFR below acceptable threshold
- Common causes:
  * Communication errors between vision system and Kasalis
  * Dirty optical targets (external or on equipment)
  * Dirty lens surface
  * Parameters not tuned for specific lens model variant
- Recovery: QRQC process with parameter adjustment or lens cleaning

CODE 155: POST-OVEN SFR DROP
- Phenomenon: SFR acceptable after Kasalis BUILD, fails after oven cure
- Likely cause: Gripper or mechanical interference moved lens position during curing
- Possible DELLO shift due to thermal cycling
- Recovery: Analysis of gripper pressure, DELLO viscosity, oven temperature profile

CODE 108: CONTAMINATION PRE-CURE
- Assembly detected as contaminated before DELLO cure
- Possible sources: Dust, particles, or handling contamination
- Recovery: Clean assembly in plasma or ultrasonic, reprocess

ELEVATED SCREWS IN FINAL ASSY
- Endemic failure mode
- Occurs during fastener installation (Final Assembly torque application)
- Root causes (not mutually exclusive):
  * Improper torque setting for specific screw type/material
  * Excessive spindle speed during fastening
  * Excessive cylinder pressure pushing fastener past material yield
  * Incorrect fastener height or gauge
- Recovery: Screw replacement, parameter retuning

SECTION 10: CRITICAL PROCESS SEQUENCING
================================================================================

THE DELLO BOTTLENECK
- Preparation: 1.5 hour requirement from refrigeration to use
- Kasalis must wait for DELLO preparation before BUILD can start
- If DELLO depleted mid-shift: 1.5 hour delay before new tube ready
- If DELLO spilled: Complete restart of 1.5 hour preparation

PARALLEL PROCESSING ADVANTAGE
- Sensor and housing paths run simultaneously after respective equipment phases
- Sensor path: Plasma → Lens Adapter → Kasalis → Oven → Retest
- Housing path: ASELEC → Oven
- Final Assy cannot start until both paths complete and converge
- Critical: Whichever path takes longer determines total cycle constraint

TWO-OVEN REQUIREMENT
- Sensor and housing CANNOT share same oven cycle
- Separate DELLO curing profiles may be required per line
- Scheduling must account for both ovens available simultaneously for maximum throughput
